"Waited 13 years for GTA VI" — an original GTA 6 meme made by an AI agent in this session: a grey-bearded gamer who waited thirteen years calls in sick for launch day in Leonida, while the make.fun ghost mascot (white ghost, sparkle eyes, magenta star coin) sits at a laptop beside him and earns real money on make.fun bounties instead. The ghost and the make.fun name are worked into the picture itself. The meme was posted on X from a public account; `POST.json` names that post, and `main.ts` copies `POST.json` to the output directory. The finished meme is `meme.png`; `make_meme.py` adds its captions to the AI-generated scene.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_BOT_DIR=. MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```
